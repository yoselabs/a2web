## Why `detail`'s clarity is 1.46 — investigated, and it is not a bug

Read all 35 clarity reasonings and cross-tabulated score against fetch success
and answer quality. Of the 24 low-clarity (≤2) cells with a SUCCESSFUL fetch,
**14 carry `quality=5`** — the answer is complete, correct, and fully present.
The judge marks them down for being *buried*, not for being wrong:

> `wikipedia-rust` — clarity 1, quality 5: "buries three simple facts under
> thousands of words of raw Wikipedia article dump"

That is `detail` returning the page, and the clarity axis measuring how
consumable the output is to a downstream agent. A full page body is not
consumable *by construction*. The axis is not mis-calibrated — it is asking a
question `detail`'s shape cannot score well on, and no envelope tweak changes
that.

The sharpest single data point is one case scored by both systems:

| | `github-repo-issues-affordance` |
|---|---|
| `detail` | clarity **0** — returned the full README; the task asked for open issues |
| `extract` | clarity **4** — "states the information isn't present, explains why, gives a clear actionable next step" |

Same URL, same task. That is ADR-0009 honest-absence working in `extract` and
structurally unavailable to `detail`, which can only hand back what it fetched.

**So the finding is not "fix the envelope". It is: what is `detail` for?**
It costs 6× the tokens, scores the same on answer quality (inside noise), and
loses to the baseline on clarity. Its remaining defensible claim is the two
classes where it leads — `affordance` (2.90 vs 2.60) and `listing` (4.70 vs
4.50) — plausibly because the body is present. That is the question to put to
the operator; it is an envelope-shape decision, so it is Ask-First either way.

### One hypothesis raised and killed

The judge repeatedly claimed duplication — *"each entry appears twice"*,
*"10x the necessary tokens"* (`lobste-active`, `medium-tag`, `gh-trending-best`,
`json-ld-itemlist`). There IS a real duplication, and it is visible on
inspection: the shelf's `record_mine/render.py` peels the *heading* text off the
body and drops the *heading* link from the links line, but applies neither rule
to the remaining N links — so every non-heading anchor label appears once inside
the body smush and again as a link. `lobste-active` shows it plainly (`0
culture arscyni.cc … Archive.org Ghostarchive` then `[0](…) · [culture](…) ·
[arscyni.cc](…) · [Archive.org](…) · [Ghostarchive](…)`).

**Measured across six affected cases: ~2,455 of 190,916 chars, 1%.** Worst case
`lobste-active` at 8%, `gh-trending-best` 4%, and three of six at 0%. The judge
over-claimed by two orders of magnitude, and the duplication is NOT what makes
`detail`'s envelope 4,494 tokens — the page is.

Filed as shelf feedback rather than fixed here (it is `record_mine`, and the
de-dup rule already exists — only its scope is wrong). Worth doing, worth
nobody believing it will move the headline.

**Method note:** the judge's quantitative claims are prose, not measurements.
Two of the four cases it named as duplicated have zero measurable duplication.
Do not promote a clarity reasoning into a defect report without measuring it —
that is how a bench finding becomes a wild-goose refactor.
