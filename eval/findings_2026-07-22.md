# Bench findings — 2026-07-22 (post-a2kit-sunset verification)

Run: `eval/runs/2026-07-22_024912` · 29 cases × 3 systems = 87 cells ·
provider `claude-code` (ADR-0016: subscription, not metered — the
`$3.62` figure is the equivalent-cost estimate the harness computes, not
metered spend) · wall 499s.

**Purpose.** Sunset task 8.2. The a2kit sunset replaced the wire encoder
(`EncodingPlan` inference → the literal `_TSV_FIELDS` table), the error
envelope, and the composition root. Those sit directly under the clarity
and conformance axes, so the bench is the right check. This is a
**verification run, not a tuning run** — no change is proposed from it.

## Verdict: no sunset regression.

| System | n | quality | env tokens | clarity | contract | reached |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| webfetch_baseline | 29 | 2.03 | 172 | 3.06 | — | 13/29 |
| a2web_detail | 29 | 3.07 | 3233 | 0.96 | **29/29** | 20/29 |
| a2web_extract | 29 | 3.38 | 493 | 4.20 | **29/29** | 22/29 |

`a2web_extract` — the shipped `query` path — leads every system on
quality, reach, and clarity, at 1/6.5 the envelope tokens of `detail`.

### The axis that actually tests the sunset

**Data-contract conformance: 29/29 on both a2web systems, and the check
is non-vacuous.** It ran on the failed fetches too, not only the happy
path:

```
a2web_detail   contract_ok=True on 20 reached + 9 UNREACHED
a2web_extract  contract_ok=True on 22 reached + 7 UNREACHED
```

That distribution is the result worth recording. The ADR-0009 failure
envelope (`status: failed` + `retrieval_incomplete` + `diagnostics` +
`narrative` + the operator hint) is exactly what the rewritten
`error_wire.py` and `_prune_wire` had to keep producing, and 16 of the 58
a2web cells exercised it against real walls and real 404s. A 29/29 that
only covered reached rows would have proven nothing about the half of
`wire.py` the sunset most disturbed.

Clarity 4.20 on `extract` (vs 4.50 at n=6 on 2026-07-08, 3.12–3.50 for
the baseline) says the two encoder defects fixed on the way out — the
presence guard and the already-a-string guard — did not cost readability.

## Not comparable to the last full run — and why that is fine

The corpus grew 11 → 29 cases since `2026-06-11_024938`, and the
additions were deliberately the hard ones (`gated`, `spa`,
`walled-api-fake-empty`, soft-404s, bespoke walls). So the absolute means
moved for corpus reasons, not code reasons, and a before/after on
`mean_overall` would be a false signal. The conformance axis is the
comparable one precisely because it is deterministic and per-row.

## Standing weak spots (pre-existing, not sunset-caused)

| Class | baseline | detail | extract |
|---|---|---|---|
| spa | 0.00 | 1.00 | 2.50 |
| gated | 0.71 | 2.43 | 3.00 |
| affordance | 1.67 | 2.83 | 2.67 |
| comments | 1.67 | 2.67 | 3.33 |
| clean | 3.60 | 4.00 | 4.20 |
| listing | 3.50 | 4.17 | 4.17 |

`spa` and `gated` are where the product is thinnest — and where a2web
still beats WebFetch by the widest margin (0.00 → 2.50, 0.71 → 3.00),
because the baseline simply 403s. `affordance` is the one class where
`detail` edges `extract` (2.83 vs 2.67), which is the ADR-0015 index
surface (`also_here` / `other_pages`) and worth a look — but on its own
merits, in its own change, not here.

Two structural notes from the error list, both correct behaviour rather
than defects: `reddit-listing` reached `block_page_detected` through the
browser (a wall a2web reports loudly, per ADR-0009), and
`koctas-product-spa-thin-fetch` resolved `paywall` with `tier=none` —
the empty-vs-wall discrimination erring to the wall side, which is the
designed asymmetry.
