## 2. The `next_links` axis is measuring page content, not the envelope

`next_links` fell (extract 3.44 → 2.56). **This is not a regression in a2web** —
it is an unusable oracle, and that is the more important finding.

Every scored cell is a rotating listing page, and the judge is reasoning about
*that day's content*:

- `gh-trending-best` 5 → 1: *"a mix of unrelated repositories … rather than the
  actual trending Python repositories"*. Same envelope shape, different day's
  trending list.
- `lobste-active` 5 → 3: *"the included stories have comment counts of 15, 1, 4,
  14, and 5, meaning the story with 1 comment is likely not…"*.
- `hn-front` 5 → 3: penalises returning 10 links when the task asked for 5 — the
  same 10 links the previous run scored 5.

This violates the rule in the `eval/corpus.yaml` header (phrase criteria against
stable structural facts so the entry survives content rotation). The `next_links`
judge prompt does not follow it, so the axis re-judges a moving page and its
run-to-run delta cannot be read. It sits at n=9 of 42, which makes it the
noisiest axis and the easiest to over-interpret.

**Action:** re-phrase the `next_links` judge prompt against structural properties
(is every link on-page? correctly kind-tagged? free of nav chrome? does it
duplicate the answer?) rather than topical relevance to that day's rows. Filed to
`BACKLOG.md`.

## 3. Real regression: `options` is capped by count, not by bytes

Envelope tokens for `a2web_extract` over the 38 common slugs: **475 → 731 mean.**
Two cells are ~90% of that delta:

| slug | 07-28 | 08-01 |
|---|---|---|
| `listing-answer-always-leaves-an-index` | 546 | **5007** |
| `arxiv-listing-partial` | 460 | **4730** |

Cause: the JSON-LD → `RecordSet` convergence (`24c1a01`) fills `fc.record_set`
where the DOM miner produced none, which populates the `options` shelf on pages
that previously had no index at all. Per ADR-0015 that is the *correct*
direction — those pages were shipping an answer beside a silently empty index.

But `_OPTIONS_CAP = 50` bounds the **count** and nothing bounds the per-option
`detail` string. On `arxiv-listing-partial` the result is 50 options × ~350 chars
= 17KB, against 819 bytes of `other_pages` and 255 bytes of `answer`:

```
answer            255
operator_hints    760
other_pages       819
options        17,113
```

`query` withholds the page body for token economy and then re-emits most of it as
`options`. That is the ADR-0015 remedy defeating the ADR-0015 premise. The cap
needs a byte budget alongside the count, and `detail` needs truncating — the
shelf is an *index*, not a second copy of the body. Filed to `BACKLOG.md`.

## 4. Confirmed fixed: listing sufficiency on the handler path

`arxiv-listing-partial` now carries what it previously did not:

```
items_loaded 50, items_total 444
operator_hints[0].code = listing_partial
  "Parsed 50 of 444 listed items — the rest load on scroll / a later page and
   were NOT retrieved. This is a partial sample, not the complete listing."
```

Pre-fix, arXiv rendered its own listing so `record_count` stayed `None`, the
completeness phase returned immediately, and the body's own "Showing N of M"
prose was the *only* place the incompleteness appeared — with nothing structured
for a caller to act on. Both signals are now present and agree.
