# 2026-07-28 — the next_links axis reports again

**Run:** `eval/runs/axis-revival-probe` — `--only listing --axis next_links --mode detail`
**Provider:** `claude-code-sdk` (subscription, ADR-0016). 16 cells, 139 s, $0.66 reported.
**Scope:** deliberately a subset. This answers *"does the axis report at all"*, not
*"how good is a2web on listings"* — the full-corpus comparison is still open.

The fix was one field name. `_candidate_block` now resolves `other_pages` for
`a2web_extract` and `next_links` for `a2web_detail` out of a literal table,
instead of assuming one name across systems and finding it on neither.

Coverage now prints its own denominator, and the two axes that were deselected
render `— (0)` rather than a bare dash — the distinction whose absence let a dead
axis pass for an inapplicable one:

## Finding 1 — `listing-answer-always-leaves-an-index` emits no index (ADR-0015)

Both systems produced **no candidate block at all** on the one corpus case
written specifically to pin ADR-0015's *"never withhold the body without leaving
the index"*.

Two things make this worth more than a single red cell:

- **It corroborates the 2026-07-27 `wikipedia-narrow-ask-indexes` finding by an
  independent route.** That one was an empty `also_here` on a narrow ask, found
  by a hand-written spike. This is an empty `other_pages` on a listing, found by
  the harness. Same invariant, different case, different mechanism, same gap.
- **This slug is one of the four that never ran in the 2026-07-22 full run.** It
  was recorded in BACKLOG as an unrun invariant case on the assumption that
  coverage was the problem. It is not only unrun — when run, it fails.

`reddit-listing` also produced no candidate block on either system. Whether that
is the same defect or a Reddit-transport artifact is unresolved; it fetched `ok`
via the zyte tier, so it is not a wall.

**Not fixed here.** Task 8.3 of `close-silent-eval-loss` says a defect the
revived axis surfaces is recorded, not repaired inside the change that revived
it — fixing product behaviour in the same commit that fixed its measurement
would leave neither independently verifiable.

## Finding 2 — where the axis does report, the scores are mediocre

| slug | detail | extract |
|---|---|---|
| answer-carries-no-fenced-scaffolding | 5 | 5 |
| hn-front | 4 | 5 |
| gh-trending-best | 5 | 2 |
| lobste-active | 2 | 3 |
| gh-trending | 2 | 2 |
| pypi-httpx | 1 | 2 |

Mean 3.17 on both systems, over 6 scored cells each. `pypi-httpx` at 1–2 and
`gh-trending` at 2 are the weakest. **This is a first observation, not a trend** —
there is no prior measurement to compare against, because the axis has never
scored anything. Do not read the 3.17 as a regression or an improvement; it is
the first number this axis has ever produced.

`gh-trending-best` splitting 5 vs 2 between the two systems on the same page is
the most interesting single cell, since the systems differ only in envelope
shape. Worth one targeted look before drawing any conclusion from the mean.

## What this run does NOT establish

- Nothing about answer quality or clarity — both axes were deselected to keep
  the probe cheap, which is why they read `not_applicable` above.
- Nothing about the 2026-07-22 quality-0 cells. Six of those scored 0 for
  *correctly* refusing per ADR-0009 (M8 in BACKLOG); that remains open and is
  sequenced behind the corpus-schema change.
- Nothing about non-listing classes.
- Cache mode was default (cache ON), so repeat cells are not independent
  observations. `--no-extraction-cache` now exists for when that matters; the
  manifest records which mode ran.
